# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FastXcel welcoming and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help others learn.
- Do not harass, insult, threaten, or publish another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose conduct is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately to
ayush.tripathi.8757@gmail.com. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and respond in a
fair, timely manner.

This policy applies in repository spaces and whenever someone represents the project publicly.
